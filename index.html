Learning on your device

Chat Patois saves learning progress, favorites, preferences, audience selection and saved voice clips on your device. It does not require an email account to use lessons. It stores an adult/family choice rather than a date of birth. A parent PIN and recovery code are saved as salted verifiers in the device Keychain, not sent to support. Keep the recovery code privately; support cannot recover a lost PIN without it.

Adult online features

Online AI chat, translation and generated speech are available in adult mode for people 18 and older after permission. The app sends submitted text, relevant conversation history, learning settings and text selected for speech through Cloudflare to OpenAI to produce replies, translations and audio. Information you type or dictate may include personal information. Do not submit sensitive information you do not want processed by these services.

Microphone input uses Apple's speech recognition framework after microphone and speech permission. Depending on device and service availability, recognition may involve Apple's servers. The resulting text can be sent for translation. AI and recognition can make mistakes.

You can turn off AI sharing in Profile → Settings. Future AI use will request permission again. Turning sharing off cannot recall information already processed. Manage microphone and speech permissions in iPhone Settings.

Parent-managed family learning

Younger learners, including children under 13, use family mode. This mode blocks app AI registration, open-ended AI chat, cloud AI translation, generated speech and microphone recognition. Bundled lessons, comics, dictionary matches, games and bundled audio remain available. Purchases and external resources require a local parent session. Opening the parent area does not turn on children's AI access. A parent can manage or request deletion of data; the PIN is an access control, not verified consent for collecting children's personal information.

Parents should keep the device in family mode when a child uses it. Changing it to adult mode enables adult features persistently. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through an adult feature, contact us so we can investigate, locate what we can, and address the request. Please do not email a child's unnecessary personal information, PIN or recovery code.

Purchases and service records

When subscriptions are available, Apple processes subscriptions; Chat Patois does not collect payment-card details. The service verifies Apple transactions and stores a random device credential's hash, subscription-linked usage records and request identifiers to administer allowances, prevent abuse and handle retries. Network services also receive connection information such as IP addresses. Our rate limits use connection information; this is separate from our application usage ledger.

Retention and deletion

Cached AI response bodies are scheduled for removal after 24 hours by an hourly cleanup job, normally within about 25 hours. Device credentials and lifetime Free accounting remain until deletion. Subscription usage records are scheduled for removal after 400 days; they are pseudonymous, not anonymous.

Use Profile → Settings → Data requests → Delete this device's online AI data. Family learners need parent access. A successful operation deletes that device's service credential, Free request records and cached AI responses associated with it. Limited subscription-linked usage is retained for the period above to administer shared allowances and prevent abuse. Daily aggregate service-cost and registration totals have no device identifiers and are scheduled for removal after 30 days. Deletion does not erase another device's records. A failed request is not confirmation of deletion; retry or contact support.

This control does not erase local progress or saved audio, cancel an Apple subscription, or remove records held separately by Apple, OpenAI or the support inbox. Remove favorites in the app; deleting the app removes its local application data, but offloading, Keychain items and device backups require separate consideration. Uninstalling does not delete server records. Cancel subscriptions through Apple.

OpenAI's default abuse-monitoring retention can include content for up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions. Our Responses requests use store:false; this does not mean Zero Data Retention. See OpenAI data controls. Cloudflare database recovery history may retain earlier records for up to 30 days. Cloudflare infrastructure logs, Apple records and device backups have separate controls; our deletion endpoint does not directly erase those records.

Support emails are deleted 12 months after a case is resolved, except records needed for an unresolved dispute or legal obligation. These exceptions are reviewed. Contact the support address for a request concerning email records.

Requests and choices

Contact Garnel Campbell at the address above to request access, correction, deletion or to raise a privacy concern. Parents may ask about their child's information, request deletion and object to further collection. We verify control or authority before disclosing or deleting records and request only information needed to handle the request. Prefer the in-app control for device records. Never email credentials, PINs, recovery codes or full payment details.

Because the app has no email-based user account, we may be unable to identify an old installation's service records from an email address alone. We will explain what we can locate, what was deleted, and any justified retention. Applicable rights and response periods depend on your location.

External services and processing locations

Radio, film, Bible and other resources can open third-party services with their own information practices. The support inbox uses Gmail. Cloudflare, OpenAI, Apple and other service providers may process information outside your country; their applicable terms and safeguards also apply.
